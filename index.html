4 aims
FAST-ED Performance Improvement in LVO Triage
→
NIH StrokeNet · PI: Thomas Hemmen, M.D., Ph.D. · UC San Diego · 2025–Present
Four-aim program improving prehospital large-vessel occlusion detection through FAST-ED validation, risk-factor augmentation, NLP-based EMS narrative analysis, and EMS education.
Aims
Aim 1 — EMS-documented vs. neurologist-derived FAST-ED for LVO prediction
Aim 2 — FAST-ED+ with clinical risk modifiers (AF, OAC, DM, HTN, prior stroke)
Aim 3 — NLP/LLM classification of EMS narratives for stroke/LVO triage planned
Aim 4 — EMS education and field documentation improvement

Research Projects

HoloStroke — Mixed-Reality Platform for Acute Stroke Care
UC San Diego · PI: Brett C. Meyer, M.D. · 2025–Present
Collaborative mixed-reality platform standardizing acute stroke assessment and trainee education through interactive, case-based simulation. Co-authored Holo-Stroke-ii inpatient immersion publication (J Stroke Cerebrovasc Dis, 2026).
Cardiac Abnormalities in Stroke Prevention and Risk of Recurrence (CASPR Registry)
Multi-center · PI: Christina Lineback, M.D. · 2023–Present
Multicenter study exploring cardiac and cervical arterial abnormalities as embolic sources in 2,000–5,000 cryptogenic stroke patients.
Zambian Cohort of Healthy Aging and Dementia
Lusaka, Zambia · PI: Melissa Elafros, M.D., Ph.D. · 2023–2025
Population-based study of dementia and associated risk factors in Lusaka, Zambia. Rotated at University Teaching Hospital under Deanna Saylor, M.D.; performed cognitive evaluations in a Zambian community clinic.
Optimizing Coordinated Reset Within the Nucleus Accumbens to Attenuate Binge Drinking
Stanford University · PI: Casey Halpern, M.D. · NREF Summer Research Fellowship · 2018
Early translational neurotechnology project exploring coordinated reset deep brain stimulation as a potential treatment for binge drinking disorder, bridging neural signal processing, computational modeling, and clinical neurosurgery.
